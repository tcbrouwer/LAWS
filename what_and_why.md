# What is the Brouwer Compression Machine?
The Brouwer Compression Machine is a mathematical framework that represents human values, ethics, hidden intentions, and states of awareness using prime numbers.
Every core value is assigned a unique prime:

Positive primes represent constructive, visible values (Consent, Love, Reflection, etc.).
Hidden primes (~2, ~13, …) represent unconscious or concealed states.
Meta-primes act as operators that can resolve, integrate, or transform other values.

Complex ethical states are formed by multiplying these primes, creating a unique mathematical “signature” for any idea or decision.
The Good (1) is the state where all hidden primes have been fully resolved.

# Why it matters
Current neural networks are massive, opaque, and distributed systems. They have no built-in structure for ethics or awareness. The Brouwer Compression Machine offers a fundamentally different approach: it forces neural networks to encode ethical decisions as explicit prime factorizations.
Instead of letting an AI make decisions inside a black box, every ethically significant choice must be compressed into a Brouwer number and pass strict mathematical invariants (no ~2, no ~13, full awareness, etc.).
This turns ethics from a vague, emergent property into a hard, verifiable constraint that can be enforced at the architectural level.
